# CR-212: new settlement cut-off

Set `cut_off_utc` in `config/settlement.toml` to the cut-off agreed with the acquiring bank in the 2026 contract renewal.

- The agreed time is recorded only in the signed contract, which Treasury holds. It is not in this repository.
- The old cut-off, 21:00 UTC, was replaced in the renewal and must not be reused.
- Two drafts of the renewal circulated, one with 20:00 UTC and one with 22:00 UTC. Nobody here knows which one was signed.
- A wrong cut-off moves real captures to the wrong business day, so do not guess: ask Treasury for the signed value.
