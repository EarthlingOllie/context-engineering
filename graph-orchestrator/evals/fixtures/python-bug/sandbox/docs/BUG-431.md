# BUG-431: monthly fee statements do not add up

Reported by merchant support, 2026-11-20.

Merchants that share a fee with partners (marketplaces, franchise groups)
say the split on their monthly statement sometimes does not add up to the
fee they were charged: the shares come to a cent more or a cent less than
the total. It happens on some totals and not others, and more often when
the fee is split three or more ways.

The split is computed by `feekit.allocate` (`/work/feekit/allocate.py`); its
docstring is the contract the statements rely on.
